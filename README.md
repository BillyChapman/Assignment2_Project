**AI Automation Gym**

Billy Chapman

**Course:** Physics Automation Gym

**Engine:** Unreal Engine

A micro-gameplay AI experiment focused on AI dynmaic perception systems, state-driven behaviour, and audio-visual environmental interaction. Players must navigate a test environment while avoiding and distracting an intelligent enemy agents. 

**Controls:**

          - E to Throw Rock
          - Mouse to look around/ aim
          - W,A,S,D to traverse the environment

**Key Gameplay Mechanics: **

          - AI Perception (Sight and Hearing): The AI utilizes Unreal's perception system to track player line-of-sight and react
          - Forgetting logic: If the player successfully breaks line-of-sight and remains hidden past a set threshold, the AI stops chasing and resumes path

**Aim:** Test the AI's detection limits, use thrown distraction objects to divert its attention, and successfully evade chase.
